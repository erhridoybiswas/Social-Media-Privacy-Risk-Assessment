Social Media Privacy Risk Assessment Framework

A defensive, educational privacy-assessment platform. Answer 51 questions about your social-media privacy settings and receive a risk score, category breakdown, prioritised recommendations and an improvement simulation.

Ethical scope. This tool never accesses, scrapes, tracks or profiles real social-media accounts. It asks only whether information is visible — never for the information itself. No phone numbers, addresses, birth dates or passwords are ever collected or stored.

Assessed categories

Profile Visibility

Public / friends-only / private scope, search-engine indexing, discoverability.

Personal Information

Whether contact, birthday, workplace, education and family details are visible.

Location Privacy

Real-time sharing, geotagging, check-ins, travel announcements.

Posts & Content

Default audiences, stories, photo metadata awareness, sensitive content.

Connections

Unknown requests, verification habits, open direct messages.

Tagging & Mentions

Who can tag you, tag review, automatic display of tagged content.

Account Security

MFA, unique passwords, login alerts, session review.

Third-Party Apps

Connected applications, unused integrations, least privilege.

Social Engineering

Link awareness, verification codes, unsolicited messages.

Digital Footprint

Old posts, unused accounts, periodic privacy reviews.

Important disclaimer

This is an educational risk framework, not a guarantee that an account will or will not be compromised. Weights and thresholds are illustrative assumptions that must be validated before any professional risk decision.
